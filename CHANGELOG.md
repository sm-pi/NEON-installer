## 1.0.0 (2026-11-20)

First public release.

### Highlights

- **Offline voice control.** Wake word "Neon", follow-up questions without repeating it, and every connected microphone.
- **Small agents first.** Most requests are handled in under a second by rules and Neon's own small trained agent. Language models are used only for writing, pictures and open questions.
- **Fits the PC.** A standard model set for PCs with a strong graphics card, and a lighter set for 8 GB laptops and older PCs, chosen automatically.
- **Apps, windows, sound and video:** open, close, end tasks, arrange windows, system, app and video volume, and YouTube controls.
- **Browser:** click by name or position, tabs, search, open sites by name (even when misheard), translate pages, downloads.
- **Messaging in the browser:** WhatsApp Web, Messenger, Facebook, Instagram and Telegram. Messages are read back and sent only on "yes". Numbered chat lists for groups and unsaved numbers.
- **Writing:** Google Docs and Sheets, Notepad, letters, emails, research papers, LaTeX, notebooks (Colab, Kaggle, Jupyter), proofreading, "no AI slop" editing and AI-writing detection.
- **Files and drives:** find, copy, move, delete, copy text between files, upload.
- **Screen control:** types into the right box on any page, and goes through app menus step by step.
- **Learns you, locally:** your phrasings, habits and accent ("learn my accent"). You can make Neon forget at any time.
- **Voice security:** enrolled voices only, a password and recovery password, multi-user approval, and a lock or shutdown after failed attempts.
- **Also:** captions-only mode, PC health checks, notes, describing pictures and the screen, shutdown and restart.

### Known limitations

- The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns on first run.
- Voice security can be fooled by a recording of an enrolled voice. It protects voice control, not the whole PC.
- Writing in Microsoft Word and Excel hasn't been tested on a PC with Office installed.
- Windows only for now.
