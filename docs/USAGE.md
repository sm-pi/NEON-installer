## Talking to Neon

- **Wake it** by saying **"Neon"**. Greetings work too: *"Hey Neon"*, *"Wakey wakey Neon"*, *"Neon, are you there?"*
- **Ask in one breath** (*"Neon, open Notepad"*), or say *"Neon"*, wait for the chime, then ask. You can also click the orb.
- **Keep talking.** After Neon answers, it listens for a few more seconds, so you can carry on without saying its name again.
- **Say it your way.** *"Close Chrome"*, *"quit Chrome"* and *"get rid of Chrome"* all work.
- **End the conversation** with *"thanks"*, *"that's all"* or *"never mind"*.
- **Risky actions are always confirmed.** Before deleting files, ending programs, sending messages or shutting down, Neon reads back what it will do. Answer *"yes"* or *"no"*.

## Messaging

Neon works with **WhatsApp Web, Messenger, Facebook, Instagram and Telegram Web** in your browser. Sign in to them in your browser first. Neon always reads a message back and sends it only after you say *"yes"*.

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| Send a message to Sam on WhatsApp saying I'll be late | Opens Sam's chat, types it, reads it back, and sends it on *"yes"* |
| Message mum on Telegram that dinner is ready | The same, on another app |
| List my WhatsApp chats / Who messaged me on Messenger? | Reads your recent chats as a numbered list |
| Message number 2 on WhatsApp saying hello | Uses the number from the list (handy for groups and unsaved numbers) |
| Call Dad on WhatsApp / Video call Nadia on Messenger | Starts a call after you confirm |
| Send the file budget report to Sam on WhatsApp | Finds the file on your PC and sends it after you confirm |

If a name matches several chats, Neon reads them out, numbered, and asks which one you mean. Unsaved numbers are read as *"the number ending 4 5 6 7"*.

## Security and power

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| Set up voice security | Neon learns your voice and asks for a password and a recovery password |
| Add a new user | Every existing user says their password, then the new user enrols |
| Remove user Rina / Turn off voice security | Needs every user's password |
| Is security on? | Says who the users are |
| Shut down the PC / Restart my computer | Asks first, then counts down 10 seconds |
| Cancel the shutdown | Stops the countdown |

With voice security on, Neon takes requests only from enrolled voices. If it doesn't recognise you (a cold, a noisy room), it asks for your password, then your recovery password. **After three wrong tries it says "Mara Kha" and shuts the PC down at once**, or only locks it if you choose that in Settings. Save your work before testing it.

Passwords are stored only in scrambled form that can't be read back. Voice security protects voice control; it does not replace your Windows password, and a recording of an enrolled voice might fool it.

## Learning, accent and privacy

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| Learn my accent | You read 17 sentences. Neon learns how it mishears you and picks the speech model that understands you best |
| What have you learned about me? | Lists what Neon has picked up from your requests |
| Forget what you learned / Forget my accent | Deletes it (asks first) |
| Stop learning / Start learning | Turns learning off or on |

If Neon gets something wrong, say *"undo"* or *"no, that's wrong"* straight away, and it won't learn that mistake. Everything Neon learns stays on your PC; see [Privacy](../PRIVACY.md).

## Settings

Right-click the orb and choose **Settings**. You can change:

- **Wake word and listening**: extra ways to say "Neon", and how long Neon keeps listening.
- **Voice**: volume, speed, captions.
- **Orb**: always visible, or only while talking.
- **Understanding you**: the speech model (faster, or better with accents).
- **Security**: what happens after three wrong tries (shut down or lock).
- **Writing**: "No AI slop" and AI-writing detection.
- **Learning**: switch learning off, or forget what was learned.
- **About you**: your name and how Neon should behave.
- **What Neon may do**: switch any ability off.
- **Start-up**: start Neon with Windows.
